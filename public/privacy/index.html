Privacy Policy

Kovalchuk Design Moodboard · Last updated: October 23, 2026

Kovalchuk Design Moodboard is a personal, non-commercial tool used by a single designer, Anton Kovalchuk, to collect visual references from Pinterest into moodboards with the help of an AI assistant connected through the Pinterest MCP server.

What data the app accesses

  • Pins and boards of the authenticated Pinterest account (the developer's own account), with read-only access.
  • No data about other Pinterest users is collected, and the app has no end users other than the developer.

How data is used

  • Pin images, titles and links are viewed to select design references for the developer's own design work.
  • The app does not create, edit or delete pins or boards, and does not run ads.

Storage and sharing

  • The app does not keep a database of Pinterest data. References may be saved locally on the developer's computer for the duration of a design project.
  • Pinterest data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used to train machine learning models.
  • The access token is stored only on the developer's computer and can be revoked at any time in Pinterest settings.
